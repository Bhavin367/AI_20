GENETIC ALGORITHM
==================

FULL GENOME IMPLEMENTATION (ga_op.py)
======================================

SELECTION: Tournament Selection
-----------------------------

Function: tournament_select()
---------------------------


Three individuals are randomly selected from the population (k=3), and the individual with the highest fitness is chosen as a parent.

Justification:
Tournament selection is simple to implement, favours fitter individuals and maintains some population diversity through random selection.

CROSSOVER: Block Uniform Crossover
----------------------------------

Function: block_uniform_crossover()
-----------------------------------

Uses a crossover probability of 0.9. Each block of three genes (representing one leg's joint angles) has a 50% chance of being exchanged between the parents.

Justification:
Keeping the three joint angles together helps preserve coordinated leg movements rather than mixing individual joint values randomly.

An alternative blx_crossover() is also implemented but is not used by default.

MUTATION: Gaussian and Block Reset
------------------------------------

Functions: gaussian_mutation(), block_reset_mutation(), mutate()
-------------------------------------------------------------

Two mutation methods are combined:

- Gaussian mutation: Each gene has a 5% chance of receiving a random adjustment, with the noise standard deviation set to 10% of its allowed range.

- Block reset mutation: Each three-gene block has a 3% chance of being completely randomised within its allowed joint-angle limits.

Justification:
Gaussian mutation makes small adjustments to existing solutions, while block reset introduces larger changes to encourage exploration and avoid local optima.

The sigma_schedule() function gradually reduces mutation strength from 0.15 to 0.02 across generations when adaptive mutation is enabled.

MIRRORED GENOME IMPLEMENTATION (ga_mirror.py)
=============================================

SELECTION: Tournament Selection
----------------------------------

Function: tournament_select()
--------------------------------

Uses the same tournament selection method, randomly selecting three individuals and choosing the one with the highest fitness.

Justification:
Favours fitter individuals while maintaining population diversity.

CROSSOVER: Block Uniform Crossover
---------------------------------

Function: block_uniform_crossover()
----------------------------------

Uses a crossover probability of 0.9, with each three-gene block having a 50% chance of being exchanged between parents.

Justification:
Preserves groups of joint angles during reproduction, helping maintain coordinated movements.

An alternative blx_crossover() is also implemented but is not used by default.

MUTATION: Gaussian and Block Reset
------------------------------------
Functions: gaussian_mutation(), block_reset_mutation(), mutate()
--------------------------------------------------------------

Combines two methods:

- Gaussian mutation: 5% probability per gene, introducing small random adjustments.

- Block reset mutation: 3% probability per block, replacing all three joint angles with random values within their permitted limits.

Justification:
Combines fine-tuning of existing solutions with larger random changes to explore new configurations.

The sigma_schedule() function gradually reduces mutation strength from 0.15 to 0.02 across generations.


ADDITIONAL EVOLUTION MECHANISMS
-----------------------------------

ELITISM
-------

Implemented inside: evolve()
------------------------------

The two individuals with the highest fitness are copied directly into the next generation without undergoing crossover or mutation (elite=2).

Justification:
Elitism ensures that the best solutions found so far are not lost through genetic operations. However, keeping too many elite individuals can reduce population diversity and cause premature convergence.

MIRRORED ENCODING
----------------------

Function: decode(half) (ga_mirror.py)
-------------------------------------

Instead of evolving all eight legs independently, the mirrored implementation evolves only four legs. The remaining four legs are reconstructed using symmetry.

The decode() function reshapes the chromosome into frames, applies the mirroring transformation to the joint angles, shifts the right legs by half a gait cycle and combines both sides into a complete gait.

Justification:
This reduces the chromosome size from 96 to 48 genes, reducing the search space and potentially making optimisation more efficient. It also encourages symmetrical movement.

However, this introduces a trade-off: the algorithm cannot independently optimise all eight legs, meaning it loses the flexibility to discover asymmetric gait patterns.
